While I am playing other instruments as well, my main focus is on the acoustic guitar.

I love the acoustic guitar for its versatility and warm sound, which fits perfectly
in various musical styles.

The finger style technique, influenced by many early blues guitarists, allows me
to create rich and intricate arrangements, which fits well with my passion for blues and folk music.

In the recent years, I have been particularly drawn to traditional Irish and Scottish folk music.
This adds another dimension to my musical expression.

Working on my vocal expression has also been a rewarding journey, enhancing my overall musicality.
