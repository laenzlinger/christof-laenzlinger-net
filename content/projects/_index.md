A collection of my personal projects — things I build because I want to understand how
they work, from the ground up. Most of them sit at the intersection of hardware and
software: custom PCBs designed in KiCad, firmware written in Rust or C, and tooling
built in Go to tie it all together.

Everything here is open source or open hardware. I believe in sharing what I learn.
